 Stress, Less Sleep, Caffein:

1. Caffein likely Stress:

It can feel somewhat similar, but stress and caffeine work differently.
If you suddenly stop caffeine after using it regularly, feeling very sleepy, tired, or having headaches can be a sign of caffeine withdrawal. Caffeine mainly works by blocking adenosine receptors, which normally promote sleepiness. When caffeine is removed, the underlying sleep pressure can become much more noticeable.

One important point: feeling extremely sleepy after stopping caffeine doesn't necessarily mean your dopamine is “too low.” Adenosine rebound/withdrawal and accumulated sleep debt are more likely explanations.
